Our Learning Departments

Science Department

Focused on critical analysis, technology, and research. Core subjects include Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, and Agricultural Science.

Arts & Humanities

Nurturing deep communication, culture, and literacy. Core subjects include English Language, History, Islamic Studies, Hausa, and Government.

Commercial Department

Building business orientation and financial skills. Core subjects include Financial Accounting, Commerce, Economics, and Business Methods.



National Examination Preparations

Our senior students are rigorously prepared by highly dedicated faculty members to ensure remarkable performance in national and regional exit assessments, including:

  • West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WAEC)
  • National Examinations Council (NECO)
  • Sokoto State Ministry of Education Unified Assessments
